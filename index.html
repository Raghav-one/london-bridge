05On the move

Travel smart

ARRIVAL

London airport to hotel

Confirm the airport code first: Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton and London City have different routes. Save the hotel postcode and check the route for your actual arrival time.

  • Allow time for immigration, bags and a station transfer.
  • At Heathrow, compare Elizabeth line, Piccadilly line and Heathrow Express with your hotel location.
  • For a late arrival, check last services and arrange a licensed transfer if needed.

LONDON

One card or device

Use the same contactless card or same phone/watch throughout. Each is a separate payment token, even when linked to the same account.

  • Tube and rail: touch in and out. Buses: touch in only.
  • Each traveller needs their own payment method.
  • Daily/weekly caps depend on journeys and zones; airport fares can differ.
  • Check overseas card acceptance and FX charges; carry a backup.

BETWEEN CITIES

King’s Cross to Waverley

Direct trains connect central London and Edinburgh. Allow roughly 4–5 hours on board plus station time; the booked service and engineering works control the actual journey.

  • Check LNER and Lumo; read fare exchange conditions.
  • Reserve a seat, save tickets offline and bring a charger.
  • Recheck disruption the night before and before leaving.
  • Edinburgh Old Town has hills, steps and cobbles: allow extra luggage time.

Edinburgh local travel and walking

London Oyster does not work in Edinburgh. Airport tram, city tram and bus tickets follow local rules; check the current operator guidance before travelling. Edinburgh Airport has a tram connection. Use Edinburgh Airport tram, Lothian Buses and Edinburgh Trams.

06Useful details

What to look out for

Work setup

  • Confirm office address, host, building-entry process, local meeting times and dress code.
  • Test VPN, MFA and recovery access before departure. Use employer-approved networks and devices.
  • Keep expense receipts and separate personal purchases from business costs.
  • Book reliable Wi-Fi and a simple commute; carry a spare work outfit in cabin baggage.
  • Save the employer travel desk and after-hours support contact offline.

Weather & footwear

  • Pack layers, waterproof outerwear and shoes with grip. Edinburgh viewpoints can be windy.
  • Check the Met Office forecast each day.
  • Swap exposed walks for a museum in strong wind, heavy rain or short winter daylight.
  • Wet cobbles, stairs and escalators need care; keep hands free with a secure bag.

Phone, money & power

  • Confirm phone unlock/eSIM support and whether the plan includes calls. Keep the home SIM available for bank OTPs.
  • UK uses Type G sockets and 230 V. An adapter does not change voltage.
  • Pay in GBP when offered currency choice and check the bank’s conversion and fee policy.
  • Keep a second card separately and modest cash for contingencies.

Street sense, food & health

  • Keep phones away from road edges and café tables; use official tickets and licensed taxis or verified bookings.
  • Traffic keeps left. Look both ways around one-way roads and cycle lanes.
  • Check menus for dietary needs and whether service is already included before tipping.
  • Carry prescribed medicines in labelled packaging with documentation; confirm insurance includes business travel.
Last 24 hours and departure

Confirm terminal, baggage allowance, airport route and airline check-in advice. Settle hotel extras, check drawers and sockets, keep passport and chargers accessible, and leave a disruption buffer. Keep UK data active until you reach the airport.
